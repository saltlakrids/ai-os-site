AI OS

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 (Gmail added)

Who this applies to

AI OS is a private, single-user application. Its only user is its owner, who also runs it: on their own computer and in their own database project. No one else can sign up.

Google user data AI OS accesses

  • Google Calendar, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly): calendar events such as title, start and end time, location and status, to show the owner's agenda on their dashboard.
  • Gmail, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): for messages from the last 30 days, the sender, recipients, subject, date, Gmail's short preview (snippet), labels and a link to the message, to show the owner's mail in their inbox and sort it by what needs attention. Message bodies are not stored: a body is fetched from Gmail only when the owner opens a message in AI OS or chooses to save it to their notes.
  • Email address (openid, userinfo.email): to show which Google account is connected.

AI OS does not send, change, label or delete email, does not create, change or delete calendar events, and does not access contacts or any other Google data. Calendar and Gmail are connected separately; each can be disconnected on its own.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner their own calendar and mail inside AI OS (an agenda, and an inbox that sorts items into categories such as urgent or requires action, with the reason shown). Sorting uses fixed rules inside AI OS. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Where the data is stored and how it is protected

  • Synced calendar events and mail metadata (as listed above, without message bodies) are stored in the owner's own database (a Supabase project hosted in the European Union, Ireland) and are only readable by the owner's account.
  • Google access tokens are stored encrypted (Supabase Vault). They are used only by the owner's AI OS server process and are never shown in the browser or written to logs.
  • Connections to Google and to the database use encrypted HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database backups are kept on the owner's own computer and deleted after 14 days.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Calendar data is kept while the Calendar connection exists. Mail metadata covers a rolling window of the last 30 days; older messages are removed.
  • Disconnecting Calendar or Gmail in AI OS deletes that connection's stored access token and its synced data. Copies in backups disappear when those backups are deleted (after 14 days).
  • Access can also be removed at any time on Google's side: myaccount.google.com/permissions. AI OS then stops syncing and shows the connection as expired.

Limited Use

AI OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

If AI OS starts using Google data in a new way, this page is updated first. Contact: the owner, via the support email shown on the Google sign-in (consent) screen for this app, or via github.com/saltlakrids.
