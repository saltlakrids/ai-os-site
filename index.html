AI OS

AI OS is a private, single-user personal operating system: a dashboard for tasks, projects and schedules, run by its owner on their own computer and their own database. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Google Calendar and Gmail

The owner can connect their own Google account so AI OS can read their calendar and their mail: upcoming events appear on the dashboard, and recent messages (sender, subject, preview) appear in the inbox, sorted by what needs attention. AI OS requests read-only access (calendar.readonly, gmail.readonly) and the account's email address (to label the connection). It cannot send, change or delete email or events, and it does not store message bodies.
