AI OS

AI OS is a private, single-user personal operating system: a dashboard for tasks, projects and schedules, run by its owner on their own computer and their own database. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Google Calendar

The owner can connect their own Google account so AI OS can read their calendar and show upcoming events on their dashboard. AI OS requests read-only access (calendar.readonly) and the account's email address (to label the connection). It cannot create, change or delete events.
